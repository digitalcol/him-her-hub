# Him·Her·Hub for iPhone

Open `HimHerHub.xcodeproj` in Xcode. The app is the club site, with the phone’s status bar, pull to refresh, and photographs from the camera or library. WhatsApp and Instagram links open outside the app.

You still have to send it to Apple from this Mac. That cannot be done from the website.

1. Join the Apple Developer Program at https://developer.apple.com/programs/ if this Apple ID is not already in it. Apple charges for the year, and approval of the account can take a day or two.
2. In Xcode, open the HimHerHub target, then Signing & Capabilities. Choose your Team. Leave the bundle id as `com.himherhub.app`.
3. Plug in an iPhone, pick it at the top, and press Run. Sign in to the club and try a photograph.
4. Set the scheme destination to Any iOS Device, then Product → Archive.
5. In the Organizer, Distribute App → App Store Connect → Upload.
6. In App Store Connect, create the app with the same bundle id. Use https://www.himherhub.com/privacy as the privacy policy and https://www.himherhub.com as support. Add iPhone screenshots of 6.7 inch and 6.5 inch, a short description, and submit for review.

Describe it as the member app for a private club in Bangalore, not as a bookmark of the website. Apple often rejects an app that is only a public web page.
